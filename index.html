Moonlight Coffee started as a single roaster in a garage and a promise: good coffee shouldn't keep banker's hours.

We roast in small batches every week, bake our pastries from scratch each morning, and keep the lights on late for the night owls who do their best thinking after dark. Whether you're here for a 6am espresso or a midnight cappuccino, the cups are always warm and the chairs are always free.

It's a simple idea, really — a place that feels like home no matter what hour you walk in.
